mbx stats

`mbx stats` [​](#mbx-stats)

- **Usage:** `mbx stats [--json]`

Show lifetime savings, pruning totals, and estimated storage shared across workspaces.

## Flags [​](#flags)

- **`--json`** — Print a stable machine-readable report, with byte counts and nanoseconds.
- **`-h --help`** — Print help
