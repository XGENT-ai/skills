mbx completion

`mbx completion` [​](#mbx-completion)

- **Usage:** `mbx completion <SHELL>`

Generate a self-contained shell completion script.

## Arguments [​](#arguments)

- **`<SHELL>`** — Shell: bash, zsh, fish, or powershell.

## Flags [​](#flags)

- **`-h --help`** — Print help
